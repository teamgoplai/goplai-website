The problem

The footage is there.
The time isn't.

You record every game. The footage sits in your camera roll — unedited, unwatched, unshared.

Scrubbing through 60 minutes of video to find two minutes of highlights isn't something you have time for. So the best moments your kid will ever have on a basketball court just… disappear.

"Their whole season. Buried in a camera roll they'll never open again."

GOPLAI fixes that. Record the game, upload it, and get clean highlight clips for every player — before the next morning.

How it works

Three steps.
Zero editing.

01
Any phone · Any tripod

Set up and record

Prop your phone on a tripod at halfcourt. Landscape mode, wide angle. Hit record and go watch your kid play. Any iPhone or Android works — no special equipment, no app to open mid-game.

02
Computer vision · Deep learning

Upload the footage

Drop the game file into GOPLAI. Our AI — trained on thousands of basketball plays — watches every second, detecting every made basket for every player on the court. No tagging required. No coach involvement.

03
Claim · Save · Share

Highlights delivered

Within 1–2 hours, you get a Game Mixtape (every highlight combined) and individual clips. Players tap "That's me" to claim their plays. Save to profile. Share to Instagram, group chat, wherever. That's it.
